**Title:** Carbon Footprint Mapping of Wards 17–19, Khulna City

**Summary:** We surveyed 108 samples in three Khulna wards and converted fuels, electricity, water, construction materials and road surfaces into CO₂ equivalents with DEFRA 2022 factors. Road circulation produces about 78% of the emission, Ward 17 has the highest settlement emissions, and almost all housing emission comes from construction rather than daily use. Satellite land-cover maps for 2019–2022 show built-up land replacing barren land and vegetation.
